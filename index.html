About Fright Club

Fright Club is a free, just-for-fun horror movie bracket for our October work event. Sixty-four horror movies across four categories face off in daily head-to-head matchups every weekday from October 5 to October 30, and the organizer crowns a winner each round until one movie is named the 2026 champion.

Anyone can browse the bracket, schedule, and results without an account. To vote, you sign in with Google or with an email and password. We use your name and email only to sign you in and to make sure each person gets one ballot. We never sell or share your information. See our privacy policy for details.

Questions? Email acfuchsgrc@gmail.com. Fright Club is not affiliated with any film studio. Movie titles belong to their respective owners.
